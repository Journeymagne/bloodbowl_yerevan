GATA Sevens · demo

A virtual tabletop inspired by Vassal. Select a counter, choose an action, then click a square or target. To block, choose a die, a push destination and whether to follow up.

Automated rules

A 20 × 11 pitch, up to seven players per side, movement, tackle zones, dodging, two rushes, ball pickup, assisted blocks, block-die selection, pushes, blitzing, armour and injury, short passes and handoffs, rerolls, turnovers, scoring and two halves.

GATA changes included

Full league rosters, including saved improvements and injuries. Before each drive, choose up to seven players with three Linemen when available and at most four specialists. Seven is a maximum: you may field fewer. Place three players on the central Line of Scrimmage (or all if fewer remain) and at most one in each wide zone. Block only during your own activation; Dodge separate from Evasive; no first-turn touchdowns; 1 SPP for a KO; kick deviation of ⌊D6 / 2⌋.

Bench and kickoff

Roll Autumn weather, then roll off to choose kick or receive. The kicking team sets up first, followed by the receiving team. Use Bench / Field below the pitch, then select a player and an empty square to position them. Auto formation keeps your selected players. Lock both formations, nominate a kicker and select the kick target. Confirm deviation, the GATA kickoff event and the landing separately. Resolve catches, bounces or touchback before the first turn.

After a touchdown or at half-time, resolve KO recovery and choose both lineups again. Casualties and unavailable players stay out. If fewer than three Linemen remain available, field those available and choose the rest of the lineup yourself. A team with no available players ends the exhibition. The other team receives after half-time.

Interactive dice

Every roll waits in the dice tray. Confirm the throw, review the result, then choose an available skill or team reroll, or confirm the result. Rerolls require a second confirmation and cannot be rerolled again. Armour and injury use pairs of D6; block dice show their own symbols. Keep your block dice before the appropriate coach selects a face. After a push, choose Follow-up or Stay before armour, injury or ball-bounce dice. In online play both coaches see the same rolls.

Prototype limitations

Fouls, interceptions, the full passing template, long passes, apothecaries, inducement purchasing and the complete skill catalogue are not implemented. Only Block, Dodge, Evasive, Tackle, Pass, Sure Hands and Catch are automated; other skills are shown for reference. No Throw Team-mate, including during kickoff Blitz. Kickoff Bribes are tracked but cannot yet be spent. Weather currently uses the Autumn table. Chain pushes continue straight; D6 throw-ins go straight inward. Blitz blocks require non-rush movement. Failed kickoff catches bounce without a turnover. This is a playable prototype, not a complete rules engine.

Online play

Open Play online on the league site, sign in and choose any two database teams. Both coaches sign in to join a league-team room. The host plays home; the guest plays away. Practice rooms are also available without an account via npm run demo. On another device, use this computer’s LAN address. Rooms stay in memory until the server restarts; disconnected rooms expire after two idle hours. Exhibition results do not affect league records.
