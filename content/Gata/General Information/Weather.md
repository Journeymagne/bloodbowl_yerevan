## Autumn

| 2d6 | d6 | Result | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 1 | Leaf-Strewn Pitch | When a player Falls Over or is Knocked Down, the opposing coach applies a -1 modifier to the Armour roll against them. |
| 3 | 2 | Autumn Chill | At the end of a drive, apply a -1 modifier to rolls to recover a player from KO. |
| 4-10 | 3-4 | Perfect Conditions | A pleasant autumn day, and nothing gets in the way of the game. |
| 11 | 5 | Pouring Rain | -1 to every Agility test to catch or pick up the ball, and to attempts to interfere with a pass. |
| 12 | 6 | Strong Wind | If it wasn't for the winds, it would be a lovely day. The ball does not deviate normally. Instead, after placing the kick, the coach of the kicking team rolls a D8 to determine the direction in which the wind is blowing (see the Wind Direction table below). Next, place the Throw-in template over the square in which the kick was placed, with the central arrow (3-4) pointing in the direction in which the wind is blowing. The kick then deviates in a direction determined by rolling a D6 and referring to the Throw-in template. Additionally, the number of squares the ball moves is determined by rolling a D8, rather than a D6. |

### Wind Direction

| D8 | Wind Direction |
| --- | --- |
| 1-2 | Towards the kicking team's End Zone. |
| 3-4 | Towards the receiving team's End Zone. |
| 5-6 | Towards the Sideline to the left of the kicking team. |
| 7-8 | Towards the Sideline to the right of the kicking team. |
