## Casualty Table

| D16 | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 1-8 | **Badly Hurt:** The player suffers no long term effects. |
| 9-10 | **Seriously Hurt:** The player must miss their next game. |
| 11-12 | **Serious Injury:** The player suffers a Niggling Injury and must miss their next game. |
| 13-14 | **Lasting Injury:** The player suffers a Characteristic reduction and must miss their next game. |
| 15-16 | **Dead:** The player is dead! |

## Getting Even

In League Play, a player who suffers Seriously Hurt, Serious Injury or Lasting Injury
and must miss the next game rolls a D6. On 4+, their coach chooses one eligible
keyword of the player who caused the injury; the injured player gains [[Hatred (X)]]
for that keyword. Big Guy, Blitzer, Blocker, Catcher, Lineman, Runner, Special and
Thrower cannot be chosen. Different versions of Hatred can coexist.

Use the final injury after treatment. Badly Hurt, death, successful Regeneration,
or Gata's protection during a team's first three matches do not trigger this check.

In the app, record the source player and D6 in the injury window. A sole eligible
keyword is selected automatically; multiple options require a choice. The result
appears in the post-match roster and is kept as an acquired trait when the match is
settled, without spending SPP or an advancement slot. An unfinished check remains
visible in the post-match; reopening it does not roll again.
